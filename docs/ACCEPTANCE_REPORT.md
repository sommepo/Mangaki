## Reader device evidence

- Installed 0.2.30 opened a real local Death Note volume through the standard Manatan reader and rendered page 194/197 at the saved position instead of a black screen or page 1.
- Installed 0.2.34 reopened an authorised real volume immediately with the saved page rendered and no black screen. Its fork-only vertical page scrubber was visibly mounted at the right edge; the scoped process log contained no fatal exception, uncaught error, or `ReferenceError`.
- Installed 0.2.35 opened an authorised real volume with the scrubber absent. One blank central-page tap revealed the right-side scrubber and its complete current/total badge; the badge was positioned left of the rail and remained fully inside the outer-display viewport.
- Installed 0.2.36 rendered the Stats Week screen with restored grouped sessions across multiple manga. The device-local all-time endpoint returned non-empty sessions after returning none before the recovery change; the original event log was not rewritten.
- Vertical mode rendered adjacent pages in one scroll surface. A single swipe visibly crossed the page seam and the counter changed from 5/284 to 6/284 in the final build.
- The revealed bottom controls showed only arrows, page count, and scrollbar. No page-preview strip was present.
- Double-tap did not zoom. Focal zoom and pan were exercised in the preceding build; pinch and compact toolbar controls remain the supported zoom entry points.
- On an authorised real Japanese page, one text tap activated the recognised block and a second tap opened the compact dictionary at the selected character. Cover images remain excluded while explicitly classed standard reader pages are admitted.
- The installed JNI self-test returned one normalized region for the synthetic Japanese phrase and recognised Japanese successfully. Real-page multi-line merge acceptance remains user-facing QA.
- Lookup supplies exact matched-word rectangles to collision-aware popup placement and deliberately ignores the containing OCR block. This build-time path is verified; visual placement on varied real pages remains user QA.
- The repeated page-62/page-107 tap failures motivated removal of the custom fork reader path rather than another hit-test exception. The standard Manatan viewer now owns page transforms, mounting, current-page tracking and tap-zone behavior.
- One Android Back key event returned from the reader to Library without closing the WebView activity or exposing the native surface. A 750 ms native debounce prevents duplicate predictive/legacy callbacks from treating one gesture as two backs.
- Library filters are collapsed behind one compact filter button; cards appear immediately below the search row.
- Series cards and the smaller cover-based volume cards show reading progress bars. Volumes can be marked read/unread individually or in a selection; the duplicate in-page Back button and natural-order subtitle were removed. Activity remains inside Settings.
- The reader's Back-to-Library button appears in the revealed top controls in addition to Android's Back gesture; live hit testing confirmed it leaves no touch-intercepting layer while controls are hidden.
- Three consecutive Home→launcher cycles on installed 0.2.29 ended in a rendered two-column Library with `WebviewActivity` resumed. A further settings persistence Home→launcher cycle on 0.2.30 also rendered immediately. A missing host or renderer triggers bounded local reload and host/WebView reconstruction while preserving a recoverable local URL.
- Temporary rotation lock produced a 1972×1248 landscape reader while retaining page 1 and Vertical mode. Automatic rotation was restored (`free`).
- No `MangaReaderUncaught`, unhandled rejection, fork fatal exception, or process crash appeared in the post-test log sample.
